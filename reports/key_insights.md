# Key Insights – Data Job Market Intelligence

## Analysis Date
June 2026

## Dataset
[Data Science Salaries 2025](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arnabchaki/data-science-salaries-2025)  
Filtered to 41,740 entry-level (EN) and mid-level (MI) roles

---

## 1. Job Title Analysis (Entry-Level)

| Job Title | Job Count | Avg Salary (USD) |
|-----------|-----------|------------------|
| Data Analyst | 3,853 | $88,797 |
| Analyst | 923 | $95,412 |
| Data Scientist | 690 | $102,223 |
| Data Engineer | 513 | $96,006 |
| Software Engineer | 465 | $122,192 |

**Insight**: Data Analyst has the most opportunities, but Software Engineer pays highest for juniors.

---

## 2. Geographic Analysis (Entry-Level)

| Country | Job Count | Avg Salary (USD) |
|---------|-----------|------------------|
| USA | 8,138 | $107,671 |
| Great Britain | 522 | $57,257 |
| Canada | 424 | $87,617 |
| France | 116 | $49,460 |
| Netherlands | 83 | $63,162 |

**Insight**: USA dominates the entry-level data job market (81% of opportunities).

---

## 3. Remote Work Impact (Entry-Level)

| Work Style | Job Count | Avg Salary (USD) |
|------------|-----------|------------------|
| On-site | 8,331 | $102,052 |
| Fully Remote | 1,508 | $89,253 |
| Hybrid | 99 | $60,252 |

**Insight**: On-site roles pay 14% more than remote for entry-level positions.

---

## Business Recommendation

For a junior data professional:
1. Target **Data Analyst or Data Scientist** roles (most opportunities)
2. Focus on **US-based companies** (remote or on-site)
3. Be open to **on-site work** for higher starting salary
